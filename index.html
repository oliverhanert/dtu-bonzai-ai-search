Shaping the future of wind

Denmark has worked with wind energy for more than 40 years, and DTU Learn for Life has trained engineers and specialists across the entire value chain — from turbine design and offshore construction to grid balancing and energy policy.

Our courses and degrees are developed in close collaboration with industry, so what you learn can be applied directly in your work the next day.
